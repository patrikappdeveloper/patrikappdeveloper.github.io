Privacy Policy — Duzz

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Duzz mobile application ("the App") handles your information. The App is developed by Patrik (hereafter "we", "us", or "the Developer").

1. Who we are

  • Data controller: Patrik (independent developer)
  • Contact: patrik.appdeveloper@gmail.com
  • Country: Italy

2. General principle: we collect nothing

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. Everything you create — workouts, training plans, exercise logs, body measurements, progress photos, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using a local database (Android Room) and local preferences (DataStore). We operate no backend server, and your data, including your progress photos, never leaves your device.

3. On-device AI workout generator

Duzz includes an optional on-device AI workout generator. When you enable it, an AI model is downloaded once to your device and then runs entirely on your device, offline. Your inputs and the generated workouts are processed locally and are never sent to us or to any third-party server.

4. Data collected by third parties (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (including banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded ads). AdMob is a service of Google LLC and is subject to its own privacy policy. To serve ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • network information
  • device type, OS, language
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks)
  • approximate geolocation (country/city level, not precise location)

Full details: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & AdSense Data Policy.

4.1 GDPR / ePrivacy Consent (EU/EEA/UK users)

On first launch, if you are located in the European Union, EEA, or United Kingdom, you will see a consent dialog compliant with GDPR/ePrivacy. You can:

  • accept personalized ads;
  • decline → you will still see ads, but non-personalized.

You can reset your choice at any time by resetting your Android Advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID") or by reinstalling the App.

5. Data collected by other third parties

5.1 Google Play Services

If you install the App from Google Play, Google Play collects data under its own policies: Google Play Policies.

7. Sharing your information (share)

When you tap "Share" on a workout or program, the App generates a text summary locally and opens Android's native share menu. The content is created on your device and sent exclusively to the target app you choose (WhatsApp, messaging, email, etc.). We do not receive a copy.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. AdMob is configured to not target content to children (COPPA).

9. Data security

Since we don't collect data, there is no data-breach risk from our side. Data on your device is protected by Android's security mechanisms (app sandbox).

10. Your rights (GDPR)

Since we do not process personal data on our servers, GDPR rights of access, rectification, deletion, etc. do not apply to our operations. To exercise these rights regarding data processed by Google AdMob, refer to Google's policies.

At any time you can:

  • Delete all data: uninstall the App → all local data (including progress photos) is removed.
  • Reset personalized-ads consent: reset your Android Advertising ID, or reinstall the App.
  • Reset your Android Advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID".

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy over time. The last updated date is shown at the top. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice.

12. Contact

For privacy questions: patrik.appdeveloper@gmail.com
